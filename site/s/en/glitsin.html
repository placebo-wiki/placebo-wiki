Glycine: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: unproven. Glycine may improve falling asleep in healthy people at a dose of 3 g before sleep, but only by data from separate small RCTs: there are no meta-analyses, the base is weak.

unproven an extract of 650 studies

What research shows

Glycine may improve falling asleep and sleep in healthy people, but only in separate RCTs, where 3 g was taken before sleep: the samples there are small, the risk of bias is high, and the data are mixed (a systematic review of 2024).

Separate RCTs also showed a supporting action in schizophrenia in addition to antipsychotics, and support of collagen synthesis, but there is no systematic assessment of these effects.

What is not proven

It is unknown whether glycine helps older people: in one experiment with a combination of glycine and N-acetylcysteine in 36 people, restoration of glutathione and less oxidative stress and inflammation were noted, but this is one small sample.

Myths

The myth: glycine is seen as a reliable sleeping pill. A systematic review of 2024 found better sleep only in small samples with a high risk of bias.

The myth "it extends life": this effect was seen in animals; in humans there are no data.

How to take it

If you try it: regular glycine, 3 g before sleep, this is the dose from sleep studies. The effect on sleep was noted from a single intake already; courses up to 4 months were mentioned in the review, but there are no systematic data on duration. The effect itself is subjective, and much should not be expected. A formal upper limit of intake has not been set: there are no data on toxicity.

Who should be careful

Biohackers: glycine is popular in these circles, but the evidence base is weak.

Older people: the data are few; the benefit was looked at only in one experiment with a combination of glycine and N-acetylcysteine (36 people).

With medicines

Clozapine: glycine may lower the effect of this medicine.

Sedative medicines: glycine may strengthen sedation, that is sleepiness and sluggishness.
