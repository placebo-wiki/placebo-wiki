Curcumin: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: it depends. Curcumin may help in osteoarthritis as an addition to the main therapy, but only in a form with an absorption enhancer: on its own it is absorbed very poorly.

it depends an extract of 1 529 studies

What research shows

Curcumin may help mood, but only as an addition in depression, and not as a stand-alone remedy.

Curcumin may help lower body weight, body mass index and waist circumference a little, but only at doses of more than a thousand milligrams per day.

By Cochrane data, curcumin works for keeping remission in ulcerative colitis, and in osteoarthritis the effect is moderate.

What is not proven

It is unknown whether curcumin together with mesalazine keeps the disease without relapses: in humans this is not confirmed.

It is unknown whether curcumin helps in cancer: the data are weak.

Myths

The myth "turmeric in food equals a supplement dose" is wrong: turmeric powder holds little curcumin, and it is poorly absorbed.

The myth "curcumin fully removes inflammation" is wrong: the effect is modest.

How to take it

Dose from trials: 500 mg together with piperine. Choose an extract standardized to 95% curcuminoids with piperine, or a phospholipid form or a liquid form: plain turmeric powder in a capsule is weak. An effect on joint pain is worth waiting for in 4-8 weeks, it is taken in courses. There is no formal upper limit; high doses can cause discomfort in the stomach and gut.

Who should be careful

People with gallstones should take it with care: curcumin has a choleretic action.

With medicines

Anticoagulants: curcumin strengthens their effect and raises the risk of bleeding, so intake needs to be discussed with a doctor.

NSAIDs: the supplement gives an extra anti-inflammatory effect.

Sugar-lowering drugs: curcumin may strengthen their effect, so blood sugar is worth monitoring.
