Alanine: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: it depends. All the data on endurance concern beta-alanine, and that is a different substance: alpha-alanine itself has not been studied as a supplement.

it depends an extract of 786 studies

What research shows

Beta-alanine probably improves endurance in exercise lasting 0.5-10 minutes, but only beta-alanine: this does not apply to alanine itself (the alpha form).

What is not proven

It is unknown whether the effect depends on the type of exercise: this is not confirmed in humans.

The effect of alpha-alanine in humans was not studied: it is unknown whether it helps at all.

Myths

The myth "beta-alanine is the same alanine" is wrong: these are different substances; beta-alanine goes into muscle carnosine, while alpha-alanine was not studied in any work of the pool.

The myth "there are data on alanine" is wrong: they were obtained for beta-alanine, with a total effect of 0.18 in 1461 participants.

How to take it

No specific dose can be named: it is not given in the reviews, and the total amount of beta-alanine taken did not change the effect. The timing for alpha-alanine was not studied; the works only had long-term intake. The upper limit is not stated.

Who should be careful

By the data of the reviews, alpha-alanine is indicated for no one: studies of it as a supplement were simply not done.

With medicines

No known interactions with medicines have been described: there are simply no data.
