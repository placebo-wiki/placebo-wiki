Magnesium: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: works, evidence level B. Magnesium is a cofactor of 300+ enzymes (muscles, nerves, sleep); the effect and tolerability depend on the form. The main point: magnesium probably lowers systolic and diastolic blood pressure, especially with a starting deficiency, and it may help with sleep and relaxation, but only when magnesium is lacking.

works an extract of 4 337 studies

What research shows

Magnesium probably lowers systolic and diastolic blood pressure: two meta-analyses, of 2016 and 2012, find this effect, especially with a starting magnesium deficiency.

Magnesium may help with sleep and relaxation, but only when magnesium is lacking; a systematic review of 2023 studies this topic.

A magnesium supplement is considered with a refined diet, hard training, at age 60+ and with a deficiency shown by a blood test.

In type 2 diabetes, meta-analyses show improvement in HbA1c and insulin resistance.

For cramps in pregnancy there are some data.

What is not proven

It is unknown whether magnesium threonate helps "for the brain": the data are few.

It is unknown whether the ionic form of magnesium has advantages: trials of exactly this form are almost absent.

B6 is added to glycinate by tradition: its separate benefit is small.

Myths

The myth "magnesium calms you at once" is wrong: the effect builds up; for sleep and cramps it is expected in 2-4 weeks.

The myth "any form of magnesium is the same" is wrong: oxide barely raises magnesium levels and is poorly absorbed.

How to take it

Dose: 200-400 mg of magnesium per day, in the form of glycinate or citrate. The form matters: oxide is poorly absorbed, citrate loosens the stool in large doses, glycinate is gentle on the gut. For sleep and cramps the effect is expected in 2-4 weeks; magnesium levels in the blood rise in 4-8 weeks; with a deficiency the use is continuous. Upper limit of added magnesium: 350 mg per day (US IOM); above that, diarrhea can happen. First magnesium from food: pumpkin seeds, almonds, buckwheat, dark chocolate, leafy greens.

Who should be careful

With kidney failure, care is needed with magnesium.

With bradycardia, care with magnesium is also needed.

With medicines

Tetracycline antibiotics: magnesium cuts their absorption; the intakes are spaced 2-4 hours apart.

Bisphosphonates: intake is spaced from magnesium by an interval of 2 hours.

Diuretics: the loss of magnesium grows.
