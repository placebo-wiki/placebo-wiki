B6: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: works, but for a narrow indication. Vitamin B6 probably reduces nausea and vomiting in pregnant women; as a remedy for the nerves by itself it has not been studied, and high doses themselves harm the nerves.

works an extract of 742 studies

What research shows

Pyridoxine probably reduces nausea and vomiting in pregnancy: according to a 2023 meta-analysis, the symptoms decreased (Rhode's 0.78, PUQE 0.75).

What is not proven

In neuropathy pyridoxine may be used only as part of treatment with dose control: as monotherapy it has not been studied, and its role is not confirmed.

Myths

Myth: "B6 strengthens the nerves". In the 2023 systematic review there are no data on B6 as monotherapy for neuropathy. Myth: "the more B6, the better". In fact the opposite: high levels give sensory axonal neuropathy, the dose decides.

How to take it

No specific dose is given in the abstracts: for nausea of pregnancy pyridoxine is taken by the RCT schemes from the 2023 meta-analysis and only under a doctor's supervision. Improvement comes during intake, the course: for the period of nausea and vomiting symptoms. No upper limit has been set, but high doses are neurotoxic, megadoses must not be taken.

Who should be careful

In pregnancy the dose is chosen by a doctor; high doses are dangerous by themselves: an excess of pyridoxine causes sensory axonal neuropathy, and the symptoms improve after stopping.

With medicines

There are no known interactions of pyridoxine with medicines.
