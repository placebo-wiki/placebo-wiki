Ginseng: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: works, but not in every area. American ginseng probably helps with fatigue during cancer therapy (the ASCO 2024 recommendation). The main condition: in healthy people the effect is small, and for memory the data are not confirmed.

works an extract of 759 studies

What research shows

American ginseng probably helps with fatigue during cancer therapy: this is temporary support for the time of treatment, the ASCO 2024 recommendation.

The 2024 update of the ASCO recommendations on fatigue in adult survivors of cancer considered American ginseng.

What is not proven

It is unknown whether ginseng helps memory: in a meta-analysis the improvement in memory was significant (SMD 0.19, at high doses 0.33), but the level of this effect in the database: not confirmed.

Ginseng may help tolerate exertion, but only as a historical use: oxygen consumption does not grow with it, and there are few reliable confirmations.

Myths

Myth: "ginseng improves all thinking". In fact for memory the data are contradictory: in a meta-analysis the effect was significant, but overall it is not confirmed, and attention and executive functions do not change.

Myth: "any ginseng is the same". In fact fatigue has been studied specifically with American ginseng.

Myth: "adaptogens are useful for everyone". In fact in healthy people the data are weak.

How to take it

The exact dose is not given in the abstracts: the 2024 meta-analysis used ginseng preparations, high doses give a greater effect on memory (SMD 0.33). No upper limit has been set. It is used in a course by the RCT schemes, usually weeks or months; for fatigue during cancer therapy by the ASCO scheme. For memory Asian ginseng (Panax ginseng) is used, for fatigue in cancer therapy: American (Panax quinquefolius); choose standardized extracts, doses by the schemes of the studies.

Who should be careful

People with cancer: taking ginseng is agreed with an oncologist.

Healthy people hardly need the supplement: the effect in the healthy is small, and data on safety in them are limited.

With medicines

Anticoagulants: ginseng may affect blood clotting.

Insulin and glucose-lowering drugs: ginseng strengthens the hypoglycemic effect, that is, the lowering of blood sugar.

Caffeine: ginseng strengthens the stimulation.
