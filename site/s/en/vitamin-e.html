Vitamin E: does it work, and how to take it

Vitamin E is far from needed by everyone: for healthy people it does not help prevent heart disease and cancer.

it depends an extract of 2 397 studies

What research shows

In non-alcoholic fatty liver disease in adults, vitamin E probably improves liver enzymes and histology, but it must be used by a doctor's prescription.

What is not proven

It is unknown whether vitamin E helps prevent heart and vessel disease and lower mortality: the effect is not confirmed.

It is unknown whether vitamin E helps memory and thinking in Alzheimer's disease and mild cognitive impairment: no clinically significant effect was found.

Myths

Myth: vitamin E as an antioxidant protects from everything. In Alzheimer's disease, mild cognitive impairment and for the prevention of heart and vessel disease there is no effect.

Myth: the higher the dose, the better. Doses above 400 IU give no benefit.

How to take it

In supplements do not exceed 400 IU, and it is better to get the vitamin from food; the form of the supplement is not critical: the data do not separate the forms. Upper limit: 1000 mg per day counted as alpha-tocopherol. The effect is delayed: in fatty liver disease courses lasted up to 2 years, so there will be no quick result.

Who should be careful

Healthy people without a deficiency: for the prevention of heart disease and cancer there is no effect, so taking the supplement just in case is not worth it.

Everyone planning doses above 400 IU per day: in some meta-analyses such doses were linked to a rise in mortality from all causes.

With medicines

Anticoagulants: vitamin E strengthens their effect and the risk of bleeding grows, so the intake must be agreed with a doctor.

Statins: vitamin E may lower their effect, though the data are disputed.
