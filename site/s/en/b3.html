B3: does it work, and how to take it

Niacinamide: verdict: it depends, the effect has been shown only in people with a history of skin cancer. In them, taking it probably reduces the risk of non-melanoma skin cancer, while in healthy people the effect has not been shown.

it depends an extract of 2 106 studies

What research shows

Niacinamide probably reduces the risk of non-melanoma skin cancer in people with such a history: in a meta-analysis the relative risk was 0.50, but only for high-risk groups.

In the meta-analysis the effect on skin cancer and actinic keratoses was significant, and adverse events in the gut are possible.

What is not proven

It is unknown whether niacinamide helps lighten dark spots: even in high-quality studies the effect in humans has not been confirmed.

It is unknown whether healthy people need it for metabolism: there are no data for everyone at large.

Myths

The myth "the more, the healthier" is wrong: niacinamide has adverse events in the gut.

The myth "the vitamin is needed by everyone for the skin" is wrong: the evidence has been collected only for high-risk groups.

The myth about lowering LDL is wrong: those data refer to niacin and a meta-analysis about statins, which has nothing to do with niacinamide.

How to take it

The exact dose for chemoprophylaxis is not given in the abstract: the scheme is taken from the full text and used under a doctor's supervision, and what is used is niacinamide, not niacin, which can cause flushing. It is taken long term, in a course, for as long as prevention is relevant. No upper limit has been set: the abstracts do not report one.

Who should be careful

Healthy people without a history of skin cancer have no reason to take niacinamide for prevention: the effect has not been shown in them.

With a history of skin cancer, take it only as prescribed by a doctor: the risk of adverse events in the gut is higher, and niacin forms cause flushing.

With medicines

Medicines: no known interactions of niacinamide with drugs have been described, there are no data.
