Serine: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: it depends (evidence level C).

it depends an extract of 1 110 studies

What is not proven

It is unknown whether serine helps children with GRIN2B variants: the studies included only 4 children.

It is unknown whether serine helps in hereditary neuropathy: in the RCT, the improvement was 1.5 points on a scale in 18 patients with a rare disease.

No significant effect on secondary outcomes was shown in the studies.

Myths

The myth: serine works as a nootropic: in the RCT, the effect was 1.5 points on a scale in 18 patients with a rare disease. The myth: it suits everyone: the dose is in hundreds of milligrams per kilogram of body weight, so taking it on your own is not reasonable.

How to take it

In the RCT for hereditary neuropathy HSAN1, 400 mg per kilogram of body weight per day was used for a year; the upper limit is not stated in the abstracts; the studied form: oral L-serine; taking it on your own makes no sense, the doses are calculated for specific genetic diagnoses.

Who should be careful

Healthy people: there is no general recommendation for them; serine should be taken only by patients with a confirmed pathology (HSAN1 or GRIN2B variants) and under a doctor's supervision.

With medicines

Serine has no known interactions with medicines: there are no data on this.
