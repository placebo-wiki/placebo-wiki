Vitamin K2: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: it depends (evidence level C).

it depends an extract of 804 studies

What research shows

Vitamin K2 probably raises the bone mineral density of the lumbar spine and forearm, but only in women with postmenopausal osteoporosis: in women without osteoporosis there is no effect.

What is not proven

Vitamin K2 may help bones and vessels, but only in separate contexts: there are still few RCTs.

It is unknown whether vitamin K2 lowers the risk of fractures: the RR figures of 0.43-0.50 appeared only after a sensitivity analysis.

Myths

The myth "bones for everyone": without osteoporosis bone mineral density does not change. The myth "cleans vessels": there are few data. The myth "works on blood like warfarin": on the contrary, with warfarin this supplement is contraindicated.

How to take it

Dose from studies: 100-200 micrograms per day, take with fats; the effect is long-term: bone density changes over the long run, fast strengthening of bones should not be expected; a formal upper limit is not set, there are no toxicity data.

Who should be careful

Women without osteoporosis and everyone who just wants to strengthen bones: the supplement is not needed, they get no effect.

With medicines

Warfarin: taking it is contraindicated, because menaquinone is an antagonist of warfarin and lowers INR.
