Glutathione: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: it depends. Glutathione probably supports cell protection and the liver, but by mouth it is poorly absorbed, so the oral effect is weak.

it depends an extract of 986 studies

What research shows

Glutathione probably supports cell protection and the work of the liver.

Glutathione probably takes part in the neutralization of foreign substances (xenobiotics).

What is not proven

It is unknown whether glutathione helps immune function: this has not been confirmed in humans.

Myths

The myth "whitens the skin" is wrong: this is a popular belief in Asia, but there are no randomized studies.

The myth "improves everything" is wrong: this is an exaggeration, and the precursor NAC often works better and costs less.

How to take it

Dose: 500-1000 mg per day by mouth, a course of 8-12 weeks. Oral glutathione is poorly absorbed, most of it is destroyed in the stomach and gut, so the liposomal form or the precursor NAC is preferred. Expect changes in lab tests after 4-8 weeks; the effect on well-being is not noticeable. No formal upper limit has been set, doses up to two grams per day are well tolerated.

Who should be careful

In pregnancy glutathione is avoided without a doctor's consultation.

With medicines

Glutathione has no known interactions with medicines.
