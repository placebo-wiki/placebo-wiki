Caffeine: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: works. Caffeine raises alertness and reaction speed; the main condition: take it in advance, before the task, and remember that in the second half of the day it harms sleep.

works an extract of 3 251 studies

What research shows

Caffeine raises alertness and reaction speed.

Caffeine improves results in time trials, for example in cycling and running.

With sleep loss and shift work, caffeine cuts the number of errors and raises attention.

Myths

The myth "coffee dehydrates" is wrong: moderate diuresis does not lead to dehydration.

The myth "after 18:00 coffee is fine for everyone" is wrong: the CYP1A2 genotype decides, and sleep suffers even if the person does not feel it.

How to take it

Dose: 100-200 mg 30-60 minutes before the task. The effect starts after 30-60 minutes, peaks after 1-2 hours, half-life is 5-6 hours. Upper limit by EFSA: 200 mg at once; in pregnancy no more than 200 mg per day. Tablets give an exact dose before a workout; in coffee the dose floats.

Who should be careful

Pregnant women: no more than 200 mg per day.

Teenagers need care.

In people with the slow CYP1A2 genotype caffeine acts more strongly: in about 30% of people the half-life stretches to 9-10 hours, so caffeine after 14:00 harms their sleep.

With medicines

MAOI antidepressants: together with caffeine a hypertensive crisis is possible.

Theophylline: caffeine strengthens its toxicity.

Anticoagulants: caffeine may affect blood clotting.
