GABA: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: unproven (evidence level D).

unproven an extract of 1 617 studies

What is not proven

It is unknown whether GABA helps with stress: the evidence is limited, and the effect is not stable.

GABA may help with sleep, but only by very limited data, which do not confirm the effect.

The claimed effect on sleep in humans is not confirmed.

Myths

The myth "GABA calms like a pharmacy drug": in fact, the evidence is limited, and some RCTs showed no effect. The myth "GABA gets into the brain": in fact, it crosses the blood-brain barrier worst of all.

How to take it

Studies used 100-300 mg; no formal upper limit has been set, there are no data on toxicity; the effect is not stable: for stress the data are limited, for sleep very limited, so it should not be expected.

Who should be careful

Healthy people do not need the supplement: who may need it has not been defined.

With medicines

Blood pressure medicines (antihypertensives): GABA may strengthen their effect.

Sedatives: GABA may strengthen sedation.
