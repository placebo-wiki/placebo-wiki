B12: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: it depends. This vitamin may help with energy and cognition, but only with a deficiency, for example in vegans and people over 60.

it depends an extract of 1 442 studies

What research shows

The vitamin may bring hemoglobin and homocysteine back to normal, but only with a deficiency.

Oral intake is as effective for keeping up the level as injections, according to a Cochrane review.

What is not proven

For cognitive function in healthy people there is no effect: in the healthy, cognition and fatigue do not change; 16 RCTs showed this.

Myths

Myth: "raises energy in everyone". In fact, in healthy people cognition and fatigue do not change; 16 RCTs showed this.

Myth: "only injections work". In fact, oral intake is just as effective.

How to take it

Dose: 500-1000 mcg orally or sublingually, with a deficiency or for vegans. With a deficiency, restoring the level takes weeks; there will be no quick surge of energy. No formal upper limit has been set: there are no data on toxicity.

Who should be careful

With pernicious anemia oral intake does not fit: only injections are needed.

With medicines

Metformin cuts the absorption of the vitamin, so the level needs monitoring.

Proton pump inhibitors cut the absorption of the vitamin, so the risk of deficiency in those who take them is higher.
