Collagen: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: works. Collagen probably improves skin elasticity and supports joints under load, but the effect should not be expected earlier than 8-12 weeks.

works an extract of 3 167 studies

What research shows

Collagen probably improves skin elasticity and supports joints under load.

Collagen probably improves skin elasticity and hydration and reduces wrinkle depth: RCTs on 1125 and 805 people showed this.

What is not proven

Collagen may help wound healing and dermal density, but the data are still preliminary.

For joints in osteoarthritis the data are contradictory, so it is unknown whether collagen helps with this diagnosis.

Myths

The myth "gelatin is just as good" is wrong: peptides are absorbed better.

The myth "it works at once" is wrong: a minimum of 8-12 weeks of use.

How to take it

Dose: 5-10 g of hydrolyzate per day. Form: hydrolyzate or peptides, not food gelatin; types I and III for skin, type II for joints. Expect the effect for skin after 8-12 weeks, for joints after 12-24 weeks; a course lasts 8-12 weeks. Upper limit: 15 grams per day is safe; above that, gut discomfort is possible.

Who should be careful

Doses above 15 grams per day can cause gut discomfort, so raising the dose above the upper limit is not worth it.

The quality of skin RCTs is often low: a high risk of systematic error.

With medicines

No known interactions of collagen with medicines have been found.
