EPA: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: works. EPA probably lowers triglycerides and eases depression, but for depression you need formulas with an EPA share of at least 60% at a dose of up to 1 gram per day, and for lipids a total omega-3 dose above 2 grams per day.

works an extract of 1 039 studies

What research shows

EPA probably reduces the symptoms of depression: in a meta-analysis the effect size was 0.28 on the SMD scale, and for pure EPA formulas 0.50.

EPA probably lowers triglycerides and non-HDL, and the effect is linear: the higher the omega-3 dose above 2 grams per day, the stronger the effect; 90 RCTs showed this.

What is not proven

Formulas without EPA give no meaningful improvement for mental outcomes: the effect is linked to EPA itself, so a supplement may help only with a high share of EPA in the formula.

Myths

The myth "any omega-3 is the same" is wrong: pure DHA did not improve depression in a meta-analysis.

The myth "omega-3 lowers cholesterol" is wrong: triglycerides and non-HDL go down, and for LDL the relationship is J-shaped.

The myth "one gram per day is enough for everyone" is wrong: for lipids you need more than 2 grams per day.

How to take it

Dose from the fact sheet: for depression no more than 1 gram of EPA per day from forms with an EPA share of at least 60%, for lipids a total omega-3 dose above 2 grams per day. For lipids the effect is dose-dependent and builds up over a course of weeks; for depression expect weeks or months of intake, the effect is not acute. No upper limit has been set: the study abstracts do not report one.

Who should be careful

Those who plan to take high doses of the supplement (more than 2-4 grams per day) need to consult a doctor: the safety of high doses was not examined in detail in the studies.

With medicines

Anticoagulants: together with high doses (more than 2-4 grams per day) you need to consult a doctor.

According to the fact sheet, no known interactions with medicines have been recorded.
