Potassium: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: it depends (evidence level B). Potassium lowers blood pressure, and more strongly in people with hypertension, but the link is U-shaped: with an excess of potassium the pressure, on the contrary, rises.

it depends an extract of 3 513 studies

What research shows

Potassium probably lowers systolic and diastolic blood pressure.

Potassium probably works more strongly in subgroups with hypertension: in them the drop in pressure is clearer.

With an excess of potassium the pressure probably rises: the link with pressure is U-shaped.

What is not proven

It is unknown what the best duration of potassium intake is: the meta-analysis included only RCTs lasting from 4 weeks, and a shorter course is not stated in the abstracts.

Myths

The myth "potassium pills are the same as diet" is wrong: part of the data concerns high-potassium salt, not supplements.

The myth "the more, the better" is wrong: the pressure already rises when the difference in potassium excretion is above roughly 80 mmol per day.

The myth "potassium helps everyone" is wrong: the effect is stronger in people with hypertension.

How to take it

The meta-analysis looked at doses of 30-140 mmol per day; no specific intake schemes are given in the abstracts. The source of potassium matters more than a supplement: taking it on your own is dangerous with blood pressure medicines and with kidney diseases. The effect should not be expected earlier than 4 weeks: the meta-analysis included only RCTs of this duration and longer. The upper limit is not stated in the abstracts, but the meta-analysis shows a rise in pressure when the difference in excretion is above roughly 80 mmol per day.

Who should be careful

For people with kidney diseases, taking potassium on their own is dangerous: the source of potassium and the dose must be set by a doctor.

In people with cystinuria, potassium is not prescribed as a supplement: for this condition, hydration and making the urine alkaline are recommended, without adjusting the dose.

With medicines

Blood pressure medicines: in the meta-analysis, a rise in pressure with high potassium excretion was noted only in patients on drug therapy, so potassium supplements together with such medicines are discussed with a doctor.
