Choline: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: it depends (grade C). Choline is a source of acetylcholine and methyl groups: the form α-GPC improves cognition in age-related and vascular disorders by a meta-analysis of 2023, while in healthy people with a normal choline status there is no evidence of benefit.

it depends an extract of 894 studies

What research shows

Choline in the form of α-GPC probably improves cognitive outcomes in adults with cognitive disorders: in a meta-analysis the difference was MD 1.72 in combination with donepezil.

Choline may help improve function and behavior (MD 0.79 and minus 7.61 on the scales), but only in patients with cognitive impairment, not in healthy people.

What is not proven

It is unknown whether a sufficient intake of choline lowers the risk of neural tube defects in the fetus: this has not been proven in humans.

Myths

The myth "choline makes everyone smarter" is wrong: in a meta-analysis of 2023 the effect was shown in patients with cognitive disorders, not in the healthy.

The myth "soy lecithin is enough" is wrong: forms of choline are different, and the data were mostly obtained for the form α-GPC.

The myth "food gives the norm" is wrong for everyone: by a review of 2025 pregnant women often do not get enough choline from the diet.

How to take it

The exact doses are not stated in the abstracts of the studies: in the clinic α-GPC is used in doses of the order of hundreds of milligrams per day, which needs to be checked against the full texts of the works. The studied form: α-GPC; in pregnancy the dose is set by a doctor. No effect should be expected at once: by the meta-analysis of 2023 the action on cognition shows over a course of weeks or months, especially in combination with donepezil, and no acute action has been shown in the healthy. No upper limit has been set: it is not given in the abstracts.

Who should be careful

Pregnant and breastfeeding women need choline only in the dose recommended by a doctor: safety was not examined separately in the abstracts.

Healthy people with a normal choline status do not need the supplement for cognition: there is no evidence of benefit for them.

With medicines

Choline has no known interactions with medicines: there are no data on them in the card.
