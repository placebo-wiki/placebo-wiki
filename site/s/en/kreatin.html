Creatine: does it work, and how to take it

Verdict: works (evidence level A). Creatine helps memory, attention, speed of information processing and strength, and gains in lean mass and strength are possible, but only together with strength training.

works an extract of 1 224 studies

What research shows

Creatine helps working memory under load and strength.

Creatine helps memory, attention and speed of information processing.

Creatine may help with gains in lean mass and strength, but only together with strength training.

It is especially needed by strength and sprint athletes, vegetarians and vegans (muscles hold less creatine without meat), and also by older people against the loss of muscle mass.

Myths

The myth "creatine hits the kidneys of healthy people": at standard doses this is not confirmed.

The myth "without loading creatine does not work": in fact it works, the effect just comes more slowly.

How to take it

Dose: 3-5 g of monohydrate per day, form: powder. Monohydrate is the only proven form; exotic forms and "transport systems" are not needed. Expect the effect after 2-4 weeks of regular use, or in 5-7 days with loading; creatine gives no quick "surge" and can be taken continuously. There is no formal upper limit: 3-5 g per day is safe long term, and above 10 grams per day discomfort in the stomach and gut is possible. In the first weeks water retention of 0.5-2 kg is possible; this is normal.

With medicines

Diuretics (water pills): there is a risk of dehydration, so you need to drink more water.

NSAIDs (for example, ibuprofen): with long-term use the load on the kidneys grows.
